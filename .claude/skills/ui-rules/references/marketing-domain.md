# Markedsføring domain map

Markedsføring (`/company/marketing/*`, admins of companies with the MARKETING product) on the app-wide rules in `../SKILL.md`, following the booking blueprint (`booking-domain.md`). This file holds only what is specific to marketing.

The user approved the domain's Oversikt and its two lists on 2026-11-18 from a sketch of the domain. On 2026-11-19 the user split the domain by channel: E-post and SMS each have their own pages (Kampanjer | Mottakere and a create page for that channel only), and the Oversikt leads to the two channels. The unsubscribe page for customers (`/u/:token`) is public and not part of the company domain.

## What the domain is about

Every customer says yes or no to **e-post and SMS separately** while booking (`marketing.marketing_consents`, one row per company, channel and address, `OPTED_IN` / `OPTED_OUT`). A campaign goes to one channel. So numbers about people are per channel, and "Mottakere" without a channel counts each customer once (dev data on 2026-11-18: 40 customers, 29 e-post, 18 SMS, 7 both).

The domain colour is pink (sidebar tile, bottom menu wash, page background). The channels keep their own colours wherever they show: `marketing-email` (blue) and `marketing-sms` (plum). Routes and labels per channel live in one place: `app/routes/company/marketing/_utils/channels.ts`; the pages are shared components with the channel as a prop (`_components/channel-*-page.tsx`, `_create/`).

## The domain's Oversikt (the Markedsføring page)

- Two key destinations side by side: E-post (blue) and SMS (plum), each "n mottakere": the customers who said yes to that channel, the channel's Mottakere total.
- No rows below and no create button (Ny e-post / Ny SMS live on each channel's Kampanjer page).
- Later areas (a Statistikk Tall page: sent, failed, opted out per month) get a row here when they exist.

## Up

- Kampanjer and Mottakere of a channel are one area: both go up to Markedsføring.
- Ny e-post / Ny SMS go up to their channel's Kampanjer.
- A campaign's own page (`/company/marketing/:id`) goes up to its channel (`handle.up` from the loaded campaign).

## Backend

- `GET /marketing-service/company-user/marketing/recipients?channel=EMAIL|SMS&page&size` (`CompanyUserMarketingRecipientController`): one `MarketingRecipientDto` per customer (`userId`, `givenName`, `familyName`, `channels: [{ channel, address, since }]`), newest yes first, paged; `meta.pagination.totalElements` is the number of customers (for the channel, when given).
- Campaign list (`?channel=EMAIL|SMS` for one channel, added 2026-11-19), detail, create, preview, cancel and messages: `CompanyUserMarketingCampaignController`.
